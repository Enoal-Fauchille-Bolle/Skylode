**Status:** accepted

## Decision

The free geometric re-roll is knowingly left open at MVP

## Why

Wiggling the dial re-rolls the layout of the remaining cells, which a patient player
could use to line the valuable ones up under an Explosive. Single-player, offline, no
leaderboard: the only person cheated is the cheater. Closes by deferring the dial to the
next regeneration if it ever bites.
