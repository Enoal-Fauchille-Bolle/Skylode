# 0110 — Prestige opens with p from Stats; the typed PRESTIGE confirm stays

**Status:** accepted

## Decision

**Prestige opens with `p` from Stats; the typed `PRESTIGE` confirm stays**

## Why

Two halves of one call. `p` rather than a panel-focus model, because that would be a
navigation concept used exactly once, on one screen, to save a letter — and it would
have to fight the `↑↓` already spent scrolling the history. A lowercase key is enough
because `p` opens only a read-only preview: the guard rail belongs to the act, not to
the door to information about the act. And the typed confirm stays because the entire
design — free reversible compression, a free dial, `Not yet` — trains the player that
nothing is final. The confirm has to *break* that training, and a `No / Yes` is the very
widget it was built on.
