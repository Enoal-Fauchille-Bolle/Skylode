**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** ui
**Supersedes:** —

## Decision

Scope is 15 states plus a toast component, not 5 screens

## Why

[DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) lists five. Nine more are specified elsewhere and listed
nowhere: main menu, terminal-too-small, save recovery, offline summary, level-up loot,
compression dialog, prestige preview, prestige confirm, and Settings. They were already
required; counting them late would have doubled the work mid-way.
