## Decision

Diagram-tool wireframes (Excalidraw) for screen layouts

## Why

A wireframe not drawn in monospace cannot prove the content fits 80×24, which is the
only question a TUI wireframe exists to answer. Kept for flows, where free-form drawing
genuinely beats text — as Mermaid.
