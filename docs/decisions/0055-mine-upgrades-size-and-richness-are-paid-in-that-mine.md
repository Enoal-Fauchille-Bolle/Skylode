# 0055 — Mine upgrades (size and richness) are paid in that mine's own material

**Status:** accepted
**Amended:** once
**Tags:** mines
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

Mine upgrades (size and richness) are paid in that mine's own material; on the three
two-material mines **both tracks** shift from common toward rare as they climb

## Why

Trades an arithmetic brake ("enriching dries up the currency that buys enrichment") for
a decisional one: high richness competes directly with prestige for Amethyst. See
[MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md#mine-upgrade-costs). 

## Amendments

### the sliding mix covers size too

Replaced: the sliding common-to-rare shift applied to richness only.

It now covers size as well ([0047](0047-mine-size-is-per-mine-3x3-to-20x10-max-upgraded-with.md)),
and the same ramp prices Netherite's Efficiency 6→15 and the End's enchants. One rule —
*the rare share climbs with the step* — for all four composite prices in the game,
instead of three unrelated fractions.
