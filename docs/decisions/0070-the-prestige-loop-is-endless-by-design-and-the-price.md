# 0070 — The prestige loop is endless by design

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** prestige
**Supersedes:** —

## Decision

**The prestige loop is endless by design**, and the price no longer exists to prevent
that

## Why

A straight inversion of the reason recorded above, and worth stating as one rather than
quietly dropping. The old entry justified the doubling as preventing an "endless free
loop"; the answer to that is that the loop should be endless and simply never *free*.
Rank 10 is meant to be a **finish line, not a door** — the last of the post-MVP
achievements — so a player who continues past it must keep finding the game, not a wall.
Under two linear terms they do: the run settles at roughly an hour and stays there, so
the achievement threshold becomes a free dial (rank 15 or 20 costs a predictable few
more hours) where under the U it was unusable past rank 10.
`the_prestige_loop_settles_instead_of_walling` replaces
`the_prestige_loop_accelerates_then_turns_back_up` and asserts the two phases apart,
since a total can hold still while both halves move.
