# 0101 — Excavator substitutes one Compressed <mined material> for the block's…

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** enchants
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

**Excavator substitutes one `Compressed <mined material>` for the block's whole drop,
and Fortune does not multiply it**; the alternative "or an Emerald" is dropped

## Why

*Substitutes* is meant at full strength — the proc replaces the loot rather than joining
it — so a proc is worth a flat 100 raw whatever the pickaxe carries. Composing the two
rarest levers in the game would put the biggest burst under the biggest multiplier: 11
Compressed, 1100 raw, from one swing at the caps. A windfall that swings by a factor of
eleven stops being legible and starts dominating every balance number near it, and it
would make the Excavator's real cost a *Fortune* level. The Emerald half of the earlier
draft made sense when Emerald read as a premium currency; in Skylode it is one Overworld
material among eight, so it would have been the strictly worse side of a coin flip on
almost every mine — an outcome the player suffers rather than understands. Dropping it
also spares a second draw per proc. Refines [MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md#enchants).
