# 0027 — A dense block and a Compressed unit are different things, and both stay

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** economy
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

A *dense block* and a *Compressed unit* are different things, and both stay

## Why

A dense block (`IronBlock`, `Cobblestone`) is a mineable, tougher grid cell dropping 9
raw. A Compressed unit is minted by the player, worth 100 raw, and is never mined nor a
`Block`. Nine versus a hundred, mined versus minted.
