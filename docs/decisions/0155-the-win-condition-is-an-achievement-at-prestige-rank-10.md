# 0155 — The win condition is an achievement at prestige rank 10

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** prestige
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

**An achievement at prestige rank 10 marks the finish line, and play continues past
it.** Neither a hard end nor an unmarked loop. The achievement list that surfaces it is
post-MVP; the ladder it sits on is implemented.

## Why

The question was open for as long as the ladder had no measured shape. A hard end
contradicts an idle game's premise, and an unmarked loop leaves a player who has done
everything with nothing that says so. A marker does both jobs at once: it states that a
run is complete without closing the session or capping the ladder.

The reason it could only be settled at phase 10's second pass is that a marker is
worthless on a ladder nobody wants to keep climbing. The prestige price was reshaped to
make continuing worth doing — see
[0069](0069-the-prestige-price-is-a-sum-one-climb-s-amethyst.md) and
[0070](0070-the-prestige-loop-is-endless-by-design-and-the-price.md). The measured
ladder now settles instead of walling: the climb accelerates ×2.1 across ten ranks, the
Amethyst phase lengthens from ~20 to ~34 minutes, and the run they add up to goes
1.34 h → 1.03 h and stays there.

**Rank 10 lands at ~11.5 h** for the speedrunner and ~15.9 h for the completionist,
against ~9.6 h under the old curve — longer, but ten full runs of the game rather than
six runs and four stretches of watching a counter. So the threshold is now a **free
dial** for whoever writes the achievement list: past rank 10 each further rank costs
about another hour, predictably, where the old curve made rank 12 unreachable in any
reasonable session.
