# 0098 — A level-up's payout is exactly one thing

**Status:** accepted
**Amended:** once
**Tags:** progression
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

A level-up's **payout** is exactly one thing — loot, or a world — never both, never
nothing; levels 15 and 30 open a world and pay no ore. The **garnishes** ride on their
own rhythms and ignore the payout

## Why

The loot exists only to keep early levels satisfying. The two levels that hand over a
whole dimension need no help, and a bundle on top of a world splits the announcement in
two and dilutes the one that matters. Refines
[MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md#level-up-rewards)'s earlier "each level-up drops a
bundle". 

## Amendments

### the rule governs the payout, not the whole reward

Replaced: the rule was stated over the whole reward.

A boost charge lands every fifth level *including* 15 and 30, because a charge announces
nothing — it is a number going up, with no message to dilute — whereas Emerald is ore
and so obeys the payout rule and skips those two levels. One exclusive axis and one
orthogonal one, each modelled as what it is.
