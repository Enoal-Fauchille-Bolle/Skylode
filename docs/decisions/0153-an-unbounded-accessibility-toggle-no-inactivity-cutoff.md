**Status:** rejected
**Tags:** ui
**Supersedes:** —

## Decision

An unbounded accessibility toggle (no inactivity cutoff)

## Why

Press once, walk away: an uncapped auto-miner at full manual rate. It would void the
basic auto-miner's whole role and make the 7-day offline cap decorative. Unlike the
knowingly-open dial re-roll, it does not self-limit — that one costs patience for a
marginal gain; this one costs nothing for an unbounded one. **Still rejected, but
restricted (TUI phase 9)**: what the bound protects is the *balance* line between active
play and idle accrual, not integrity — a strip of tape over the mine key gives the same
thing, as the accepted toggle row itself argues. Any finite bound closes this, so the
choice of number is an ergonomic one, and it landed at fifteen minutes with a toast.
