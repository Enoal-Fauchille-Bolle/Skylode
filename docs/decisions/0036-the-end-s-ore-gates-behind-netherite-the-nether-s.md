## Decision

**The End's ore gates behind Netherite, the Nether's behind Diamond**

## Why

Amends the mine gating table in [MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md#mine-gating-table).
End Stone/Amethyst were Wooden-gated and the Nether's
Quartz was too, so a level-30 player reached the game's richest ore — the prestige
currency and top enchant material — with the pickaxe they started on, and the two-axis
gate collapsed to one on the shortest path: an XP race to level 30 with no tier climb,
which the phase-10 balance harness measured at ~2.6 h. Raising the endgame ore to the
top of the ladder restores the gate — Amethyst now needs Netherite, Quartz the Nether's
Diamond, so reaching either is proof of a full climb. It also gives Netherite a mine to
open where it opened nothing before (the Overworld's own ladder tops out at Iron), and
makes the End's Amethyst-fuelled enchants genuinely endgame. Both cells of each mine
rise together, so `common_and_value_share_a_gating_tier` still holds. See
[MECHANICS.md](../MECHANICS.md#mine-gating-table).
