# 0012 — The level-up bundle shares the enchant fuel table rather than owning one

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** enchants
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

The level-up bundle **shares the enchant fuel table** rather than owning one

## Why

The bundle quotes the world's enchant material at 50 % and that rung's abundant and
scarce fuel ores at 35 % / 15 % — the same materials and the same split as the [enchant
price](../MECHANICS.md#enchants). The player recognises what they receive because it is
the shape of what they spend, and a re-balance of the fuel moves both in one edit
instead of leaving two provisional tables to drift apart. The End quotes two lines on
the rare ramp, for the reason its price does: one mine, whose rare cell already *is* the
enchant material.
