# 0026 — Costs are paid in the denomination they are quoted in

**Status:** accepted
**Tags:** economy
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Decision

Costs are paid in the denomination they are quoted in: 650 raw Iron does *not* buy `6
Compressed Iron + 50 Iron`

## Why

Makes compressing a step in the upgrade path instead of a cosmetic button. The refusal
costs one action to clear, since compression is free, so the friction is a beat, not a
wall.
